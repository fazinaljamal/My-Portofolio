About Kitab Mulazzamah

Kitab Mulazzamah is a learning program that includes 3 main classical Islamic books studied at Ma'had HSI. Each book has 3 explanatory videos and 1 portfolio document.

This program aims to document the learning journey of classical books that serve as the foundation for understanding Islamic religious knowledge in depth.

Book 1

Kitab Aqidatuna

3 Videos • 1 Document

A foundational book in Aqidah (Islamic creed) discussing topics about Aqidah. The material covers introduction to Aqidah, your God, your Prophet, your religion, and the basics of Aqidah.
