About the Project

Tasmi' Project is a Quran memorization project for Juz 29 and 30 that I submitted at SMA IT HSI Boarding School. This project aims to help students read the Quran correctly according to the proper rules of tajweed.

Through this project, I had the opportunity to present my memorization under the guidance of ustadz/ustadzah who supervised the correct recitation of the Quran with proper tajweed.
